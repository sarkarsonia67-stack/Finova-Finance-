## Important production requirements
This ZIP is a front-end demo/template. Do NOT collect or store sensitive KYC documents in browser-only code.

Before going live:
1. Add a secure backend (Node/PHP/etc.).
2. Use HTTPS.
3. Store documents in private storage, never a public uploads folder.
4. Authenticate users and restrict admin access.
5. Verify Razorpay payment using Razorpay's server-side API/webhooks before marking an application paid/successful.
6. Replace placeholder partner names/logos only with banks/NBFCs you are actually authorized to represent.
7. Clearly publish your legal business name, lender relationships, fees, privacy policy, terms, grievance/contact details and refund/cancellation policy.
8. Do not imply that loan approval is guaranteed.
9. Ensure your business model, fee disclosure and lender/DSA arrangements comply with applicable Indian laws and RBI requirements.
